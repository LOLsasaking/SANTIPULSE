# 🚀 SantiPulse: Video & Package Strategy Guide

This guide explains the three-package architecture and provides specific video ideas for your `/demos/` page to replace the old forms.

---

## 📦 PACKAGE 1: THE RECEPTIONIST & LEADS HUB
**Goal:** Automate the "Front Desk" and never miss a lead.

### 🛠 Core Features:
- **AI Voice Assistant:** Real-time phone conversations (Vapi/Bland).
- **WhatsApp CRM:** Automated chat flows and lead qualification.
- **Calendar Sync:** Automatic booking for qualified leads.
- **Human SOS:** Real-time alerts when a human needs to step in.

### 🎥 Video Demo Idea: "The 24/7 Front Desk"
- **Scene 1:** Show a smartphone calling the business at 2 AM.
- **Scene 2:** Split-screen showing the AI Voice answering naturally while the **Admin Dashboard** creates a new "Lead Profile" in real-time.
- **Scene 3:** The AI asks for a time, checks the calendar, and the video shows the appointment appearing on the dashboard calendar automatically.
- **Message:** "Your business never sleeps. Let AI handle the first contact."

---

## 📦 PACKAGE 2: SOCIAL INSIGHTS & AUTO-POSTER
**Goal:** Post what works, automatically.

### 🛠 Core Features:
- **Trend Scraper:** Scans TikTok/Instagram for viral styles in your niche.
- **Metric Analytics:** Tells you *why* a post is working (hook, length, music).
- **Auto-Poster:** Queues and publishes content via official APIs.

### 🎥 Video Demo Idea: "Viral Content on Autopilot"
- **Scene 1:** Show the "Insights" tab of the dashboard scanning TikTok.
- **Scene 2:** The dashboard highlights 3 "Trending Styles" with a "High Probability of Viral" score.
- **Scene 3:** Show a user clicking "Queue" and then a time-lapse of the post appearing on the Instagram feed.
- **Message:** "Stop guessing. Use data to dominate the algorithm."

---

## 📦 PACKAGE 3: AUTO-AD MANAGER
**Goal:** Maximize ROI by letting AI manage the budget.

### 🛠 Core Features:
- **Autonomous Optimization:** Adjusts budgets based on ROAS/CPC in real-time.
- **Performance Rules:** "If CPC > $2.00, pause ad and alert owner."
- **Multi-Platform Deploy:** Manage Meta and TikTok Ads from one tab.

### 🎥 Video Demo Idea: "The Digital Media Buyer"
- **Scene 1:** Show a dashboard view of 10 running ad campaigns.
- **Scene 2:** Zoom in on a "Rule" being triggered: "Scaling Budget on Campaign B due to high ROAS."
- **Scene 3:** Show the budget graph moving up automatically while the "Human SOS" sends a "Profit Alert" to the owner's phone.
- **Message:** "Scale your profits, not your workload."
