# Business Plan 💼

## The Model

One reusable booking-website codebase → sold/customized to many local businesses. Build once, customize per client in ~10–30 minutes, deploy in seconds.

## Verticals (who you sell to)

| Business | Payment needed? | Time slots | Services |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tour operator | ✅ Yes (deposits) | 2:00 PM, 4:00 PM | Whale watching, snorkeling |
| **Barber shop** | ❌ No (just booking) | 9am–7pm, 30 min | Haircut, Fade, Beard, Shave, Package |
| **Nail salon** | ❌ No | 10am–6pm, 1 hr | Manicure, Pedicure, Gel, Design, Combo |
| Spa | ❌ No | 9am–8pm, 1 hr | Massage, facial, sauna |
| Dentist | ❌ No | 8am–5pm, 30 min | Checkup, cleaning, extraction |
| Restaurant | Optional (deposit) | Lunch/dinner | Table reservations |
| Gym / trainer | ❌ No | varies | Sessions |

> [!tip] The insight
> Same codebase → **10+ verticals** instead of just one. Remove payment for service businesses, keep it for tours/restaurants.
