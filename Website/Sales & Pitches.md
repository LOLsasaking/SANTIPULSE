## The core pitch

> "Every time someone books, you get:
> - **Instant notification** — never miss a booking
> - **Your own booking page** — on your own site, your brand
> - **Guest contact info** — name, email, phone, service
> - (tours/restaurants) **Payment confirmation** in your Stripe
>
> Everything is automatic. You just check your email and dashboard."

## Pitch email — Nail salon
